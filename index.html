AutoCare AI

Vehicle Maintenance Learning Assistant

Dev Rohitbhai Patel · B.Tech Mechanical · Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence

Project overview

A browser-based offline educational assistant that matches vehicle-maintenance questions to curated explanations using keyword-based rules. It is not a live mechanic or a generative AI system.

Problem and objective

Beginners often struggle to interpret common vehicle symptoms. AutoCare AI provides understandable explanations and highlights when professional inspection is needed.

How it works

JavaScript checks user text for keywords about cooling, starting, brakes or servicing. A matching topic displays a prewritten response; unknown inputs receive an out-of-scope message.

Topics and examples

  • Overheating and cooling system
  • Battery and starting issues
  • Brake noise and safety
  • Preventive maintenance schedules

Responsible use and sources

General educational content only. The app does not collect or transmit user questions. Vehicle-specific advice must be checked against the owner's manual and a qualified technician.

References: vehicle owner's manual; NHTSA vehicle safety.
